ABOUT // KAZI ZAYEED

“Why?” is my favorite follow-up question.

I'm Zayeed, an Honours Mathematics and Financial Analysis and Risk Management (FARM) student at Waterloo. I ask why a lot: why does this work, why is it set up this way, and who decided that was a good idea? I chose this path because math and FARM give me more tools in my arsenal to chase those answers and test what I think I know.

Finance and consulting appeal to me because they're full of interesting whys: why a decision was made, why a model works, and what happens if you try another approach. They also leave room to fail, which is convenient because I'm already pretty good at that. I just try to make the next attempt smarter.
