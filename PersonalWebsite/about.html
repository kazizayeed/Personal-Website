My philosophy

ask why, fail, learn, try again

My first question is usually “why?” The first answer is rarely the whole answer, so I keep digging: what makes this work, and what are we assuming?

Sometimes I find an answer. Sometimes I find a new question. Sometimes I get it wrong. I’m pretty good at that last one, but getting it wrong gives me something useful to work with.

From there, I build the idea back up and try again. That attempt can fail too. Annoying, but now I have better information.

This is the framework I try to use across my life: try, fail, ask why, return to first principles, try again. Repeat. I’m pretty practiced at the failing part; thankfully, it isn’t the last step.

I don’t expect every attempt to work. I do want to leave each one with a better question, a clearer idea, or fewer assumptions than I started with.
